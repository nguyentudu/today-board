## What Moon Local promises

- local-first Today Board and offline use;
- JSON export/import without an account;
- situation, context, return point, next step, promise, and outcome memory;
- no behavioral tracking or raw continuity telemetry.

Local capacity depends on the device and browser. Clearing site data can remove
the board; export before clearing data, changing browser, or moving domains.

## What paid plans may add

- client-encrypted cross-device sync;
- version history and recovery;
- extended encrypted-object capacity;
- optional continuity review and priority support.

These capabilities are not active in R1. Sign-in, checkout, customer portal,
remote storage, and paid entitlement are deliberately unavailable.

## Not part of Commercial v1

AI chat, marketplace, social feed, team collaboration, calendar automation,
engagement notifications, Proof Commerce integration, and voice productization.

Before a real payment can be accepted: provider ownership and KYC, exact live
catalog, adopted refund terms, signed webhook verification, durable ledger,
entitlement derivation, a real-money smoke order and refund, support ownership,
and production rollback evidence must all pass.
